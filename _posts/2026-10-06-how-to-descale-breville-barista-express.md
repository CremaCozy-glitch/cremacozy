You reach for your morning shot and a light you don't usually see is on. That's annoying, especially before coffee. The good news is that nothing is broken. The machine is asking to be descaled, which just means flushing out the mineral buildup (limescale) that water leaves inside.

I own this machine too, so I know that little jolt when a new light turns on. Everything below follows Breville's instruction book for the BES870, the standard Barista Express. Where the manual doesn't say something, I'll tell you instead of guessing. I also checked a few popular guides against the manual, and some of them get things wrong. I point those out below.

<div class="callout" markdown="1">
**Quick answer:** A solid CLEAN / DESCALE light means it's time to descale. Mix one Breville descaling powder sachet in 1 L (34 fl oz) of water, enter descale mode by holding 2 CUP and then POWER, run the coffee, steam and hot water steps twice, then do one more round with fresh water to rinse.

## Check the light first

The CLEAN / DESCALE light does two jobs, and it's easy to mix them up.

- **Flashing** means the machine wants a cleaning cycle. That's a different job, and it uses a cleaning tablet. Our [cleaning tablets guide]({{ '/best-cleaning-tablets-breville-barista-express/' | relative_url }}) explains the cycle and what the flashing and solid lights mean. If the light stays on after you've cleaned, try the [Clean Me light fix]({{ '/breville-barista-express-clean-me-light-wont-turn-off-fix/' | relative_url }}).
- **Solid** (on and not blinking) means the machine wants a descale. That's what this page is about.

A cleaning tablet won't remove limescale, and descaling powder won't remove coffee oil. You may need both, one after the other.

## What you'll need

You need one Breville descaling powder sachet. The manual's list of included accessories mentions descale powder, so check your box and the storage tray behind the drip tray first. If you need more, here's a <a href="https://link.amazon/B0csUW08d" target="_blank" rel="sponsored nofollow noopener">Breville descaling powder on Amazon</a>.

If your light was flashing instead of solid, you need cleaning tablets, not descaling powder. Here are <a href="https://link.amazon/B08PYo6wz" target="_blank" rel="sponsored nofollow noopener">Breville cleaning tablets on Amazon</a>.

You'll also want cold water, a towel, and a large bowl or container. The manual doesn't say how to catch the liquid, but the solution comes out of the coffee outlet, the steam wand and the hot water spout. So I'd empty the drip tray before you start and keep the bowl close.

My own tip, not the manual's: use a container that holds 2 L (68 fl oz). The rinse uses a full tank, so a big container means you don't have to stop and empty it halfway. A <a href="https://link.amazon/B0c4S4Kkm" target="_blank" rel="sponsored nofollow noopener">2 liter measuring jug</a> works well and shows you the 1 L mark for mixing too.

## The steps

1. **Mix the solution.** Dissolve one sachet in 34 fl oz (1 L) of water, then fill the water tank with it. The manual warns never to remove the tank or empty it completely during descaling. The only time it comes out is for the rinse in step 8.
2. **Enter descale mode.** Press and hold the 2 CUP button, then press and hold POWER as well. The POWER light flashes while the machine heats up.
3. **Wait for four steady lights.** When POWER, 1 CUP, CLEAN / DESCALE and HOT WATER / STEAM are all on and not blinking, the machine is ready.
4. **Descale the coffee side.** Press 1 CUP. It takes about 25 seconds. The 1 CUP light blinks while it works and turns steady when it's done. To stop early, press 1 CUP again within those 25 seconds.
5. **Descale the steam side.** Turn the steam dial to the right, toward the steam icon. This takes about 13 seconds.
6. **Descale the hot water side.** Turn the dial to the left, toward the hot water icon. This takes about 8 seconds. If you need to stop steam or hot water early, turn the dial back to STANDBY.
7. **Run it again.** After one round, about half of the solution (roughly 17 fl oz / 0.5 L) should be left in the tank. Repeat steps 4 to 6 to finish the rest.
8. **Rinse.** Take the tank out and rinse it well. Fill it with fresh cold water up to the MAX mark (68 fl oz / 2 L), then repeat steps 4 to 6 once more. This clears out any leftover solution.
9. **Finish.** Press POWER to leave descale mode. The lights switch off.

The manual's steps don't say anything about the portafilter. Don't put coffee or a cleaning tablet in it for this job, because those belong to the cleaning cycle.

### How long does it take?

The manual doesn't give a total time. Here's what I can work out from its numbers. Each round is 25 + 13 + 8 seconds, so about 46 seconds of pumping. You do three rounds (two with the solution, one rinse), so roughly 2 minutes 20 seconds of pumping in all. On top of that you have the heat-up, mixing, rinsing the tank and moving the dial, so plan on being near the machine for a while. Don't walk away. If you leave it alone for 5 minutes it shuts itself off.

## If something goes sideways

**The machine switched itself off.** In descale mode, it turns off after 5 minutes without any action. Start again from step 2.

**You stopped a cycle by accident.** Some guides online say pressing 1 CUP again makes the cycle continue. The manual says the opposite: pressing it again ends the cycle. The manual doesn't explain how to pick up where you left off, so I'd run steps 4 to 6 again rather than guess.

**The light is still on afterward.** The manual doesn't describe a separate reset for this light. Check that you finished both the solution run and the fresh water rinse. If the light is still solid, call Breville Consumer Support, because the manual says to call them for any maintenance beyond cleaning. If the light is flashing instead, that's the cleaning cycle asking again, not descaling.

**Don't use the PROGRAM button reset for this.** A few guides, and the Google AI summary, say holding PROGRAM until the lights flash will clear the descale light. In the manual, holding PROGRAM until the machine beeps three times only puts your 1 CUP and 2 CUP shot volumes and the water temperature back to default. It has nothing to do with descaling, and you'd lose any shot volumes you set yourself.

**The machine is on but stopped working.** The manual says a safety cut-out can switch it off if the pump or grinder gets too hot. Turn it off with POWER, unplug it and let it cool for about 30 to 60 minutes. If it keeps happening, call Breville Consumer Support.

**The grinder is the problem.** Descaling only cleans the water side of the machine. If the grinder is jamming, too loud or not giving coffee, that's a separate fix. See [Barista Express grinder not working]({{ '/barista-express-grinder-not-working/' | relative_url }}).

**Water isn't flowing, or the coffee isn't hot.** The manual lists "machine needs to be descaled" as one possible cause when water doesn't come out of the coffee outlet, there's no hot water, there's no steam, or the coffee isn't hot enough. It lists other causes too, like an empty tank or a tank that isn't pushed all the way in. Check those simple things first.

## Why it's worth doing

Breville explains in its <a class="ext" href="https://www.breville.com/content/dam/breville/us/en/assets/miscellaneous/instruction-manual/espresso/BES870-instruction-manual.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official BES870 instruction book</a> that hard water leaves minerals inside the machine. Over time that can reduce the flow of water, the brewing temperature, the power of the machine and the taste of your espresso.

## The water filter

The filter that comes in the water tank helps prevent that buildup. The manual says changing it every two months means you'll need to descale less often, and sooner if you have hard water. It also says to take the filter out if you use filtered or bottled water. If you need a new one, here's a <a href="https://link.amazon/B0gKEnK9M" target="_blank" rel="sponsored nofollow noopener">Breville Barista Express water filter on Amazon</a>. Breville's own manual says to buy filters through Breville Consumer Support, so check that the one you pick fits your model.

One thing worth knowing: the manual's descaling steps never say to take the filter out first. Other guides (and the Google summary) tell you to remove it, because the solution would otherwise soak the filter. I couldn't find that in Breville's book, so I won't claim it's required. If you want a clear answer for your own machine, Breville Consumer Support can tell you.

The manual also says not to use distilled or demineralized water. It can change the taste of the espresso and how the machine is meant to work.

## How often should you descale?

The manual doesn't give a calendar schedule. It says to descale when the CLEAN / DESCALE light is solid, so let the light decide. You'll see "every 3 months" in some guides. That's their rule of thumb, not Breville's.

## What about vinegar?

The manual only mentions Breville's own descaling powder. It doesn't give a vinegar recipe, so I won't make one up. If you want to use something else, check with Breville Consumer Support first.

## Not a standard Barista Express?

These steps come from the BES870 manual. Other Barista models have their own buttons and steps, so use the manual for your model. In the UK and Europe the same machine is sold as the Sage Barista Express (SES870), but check your own manual if a button combination doesn't behave the way I describe.
