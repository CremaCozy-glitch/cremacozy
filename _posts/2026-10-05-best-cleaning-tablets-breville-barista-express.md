## What the cleaning cycle actually does

It's easy to mix up cleaning and descaling, so here's the simple version.

**Cleaning cycle:** This is a back-flushing cycle that runs water through the group head using a tablet and rubber cleaning disc. It removes coffee oils and residue from the brew path.

**Descaling:** This removes mineral scale from inside the machine using Breville's descaling powder, typically with the water filter removed first.

Breville uses an indicator to tell you what it needs:
- **Flashing** = cleaning cycle (tablet + disc)
- **Solid** = descaling

On some Barista Express versions, the label is written as CLEAN ME or shown as a combined CLEAN/DESCALE indicator. Follow the flashing vs solid behavior, not just the text.

## How to run the cleaning cycle

These steps follow the instruction-book approach for the Barista Express. Still, check your own manual because versions can differ.

1. Put the **1 CUP filter basket** into the portafilter.
2. Place the **rubber cleaning disc** inside the basket.
3. Add **one cleaning tablet** on top of the disc.
4. Lock the portafilter into the group head.
5. Put a **large container** under the spouts.
6. Make sure the **water tank has cold water** and the **drip tray is empty**.
7. Switch the machine **off**.
8. Press **1 CUP** and **2 CUP** together, then also press **POWER** and **hold all three for about 10 seconds**.
9. The cycle starts, runs for about **5 minutes**, and ends with **2 beeps**.
10. Remove the portafilter and check whether the tablet dissolved. If not, repeat the cycle until it's fully dissolved.

## What people also ask about cleaning tablets

These are the questions that come up most often. They're worth clearing up before you buy or run your next cycle.

### Can I use any espresso cleaning tablet?

Not all tablets are the same. Use tablets designed for espresso machines with a cleaning cycle. If you use compatible tablets, check that the listing says they work for Breville machines and that the dosage matches a single cleaning cycle.

### Can I use Cafiza or other barista-grade cleaners?

Usually yes, but they're often stronger and can foam more than regular home tablets. If you choose a stronger cleaner, you may need to run an extra rinse cycle after cleaning. If your machine is under warranty, the official Breville option is the safer route.

### I lost the rubber cleaning disc. Can I still use a tablet?

No. The disc creates the seal needed for the cycle to work properly. Without it, the tablet can't do its job. You can order a replacement disc online. Look for one that fits Breville's 54mm portafilter.

### What happens if the tablet doesn't dissolve?

Repeat the cleaning cycle until it dissolves completely. If it repeatedly doesn't dissolve, check:
- Disc placement (fully seated in the basket)
- Basket choice (1 CUP, not 2 CUP)
- That the machine is actually running the full cycle

### How many shots until the indicator shows up?

Breville's manual uses the indicator rather than a fixed number. Practically, it depends on usage and how oily the beans are. If your espresso starts tasting off, clean earlier.

### Can I use vinegar instead of a cleaning tablet?

No. Vinegar doesn't break down coffee oils the way cleaning tablets do, and it leaves a strong smell inside the machine that's hard to rinse out. Stick with proper espresso cleaning tablets.

### Are cleaning tablets and descaling the same thing?

No. Cleaning targets coffee oils and residue through the brew path. Descaling targets mineral scale inside the machine with descaling powder. Both belong in your maintenance routine.

## Common mistakes to avoid

| Mistake | Why it matters |
|---------|----------------|
| Using the 2 CUP basket | Manual calls for the 1 CUP filter basket |
| Leaving out the cleaning disc | The manual's method depends on the disc |
| Not using a container under the spouts | The cycle runs water from the spouts |
| Starting without switching the machine off first | Manual says to power off before button sequence |
| Letting go of buttons early | Manual says hold for about 10 seconds |
| Cleaning when the indicator is solid | Solid means descaling, not tablet cleaning |
| Running with an empty or low tank | Cycle may not complete properly |
