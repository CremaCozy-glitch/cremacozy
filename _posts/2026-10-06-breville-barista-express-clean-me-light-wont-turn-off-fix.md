You ran the cleaning cycle, waited for it to finish, and the Clean Me light is still on. Annoying, I know.

Most of the time, the cycle did not complete properly, or the cleaning disc or tablet was missing. Check those first, then work down the list below.

## The three things to check first

Before you assume something is wrong with the machine, check these:

1. **Did you use the cleaning disc and a tablet?**  
   Running water through the machine is not the same as running the proper cleaning cycle.

2. **Did you start the cycle with the correct button combination?**  
   On the Barista Express, you need to press and hold the correct buttons together to start the programmed cycle.

3. **Did you let the full cycle finish?**  
   If you stopped it early, turned the machine off too soon, or the water tank ran low, the light may stay on.

If one of those happened, run the cleaning cycle again properly before trying anything else.

## How to run the cleaning cycle properly

Here is the correct way to do it:

1. Turn the machine off with the **POWER** button. Off, not standby.

2. Put the **1 CUP filter basket** in the portafilter, set the **cleaning disc** in it, and place **one cleaning tablet** on top.

3. Lock the portafilter into the group head.

4. Make sure the water tank has enough water, and keep the drip tray in place.

5. Press and hold **1 CUP**, **2 CUP**, and **POWER** together. Keep holding for about 10 seconds until the cleaning cycle starts.

  <figcaption>Hold POWER + 1 CUP + 2 CUP to enter cleaning mode.</figcaption>
</figure>

6. Leave the machine alone until the cycle fully finishes.

7. Remove the portafilter, rinse everything, and check the light again.

If the cycle ran correctly, the Clean Me light should turn off.

## Still on after a proper cycle?

If the light is still on after you did the full cycle correctly, these are the most likely reasons:

### 1. The cycle did not actually complete
Sometimes the machine starts, but the cycle gets interrupted. This can happen if:

- the water tank ran low
- the portafilter was not seated properly
- the machine was switched off too early

Run it again from the beginning and do not interrupt it.

### 2. The cleaning disc or tablet was missing or wrong
The machine expects the proper backflush setup.

If you used:
- no tablet
- the wrong basket
- no cleaning disc
- a random homemade substitute

the cycle may not reset the Clean Me light.

### 3. You used rinse mode, not the cleaning cycle
A quick flush through the group head is useful, but it does **not** reset the maintenance reminder.

Only the full programmed cleaning cycle will do that.

### 4. The machine may need another cycle
If the machine was heavily used or has a lot of built-up coffee oils inside, one cycle may not be enough.

Try one more full cleaning cycle with a fresh tablet.

## What not to do

Avoid these common mistakes:

- Do **not** keep pressing random buttons hoping the light will reset
- Do **not** use descaling steps for a Clean Me light issue
- Do **not** force the machine off halfway through the cycle
- Do **not** assume the machine is broken before re-running the cycle properly

## Clean Me light vs descale light

These two are not the same.

- **Clean Me light** = the machine wants a **cleaning/backflush cycle**
- **Descale alert** = the machine needs **descaling**

If you follow descale instructions when the machine actually wants a cleaning cycle, the Clean Me light may stay on.

## If the light still will not turn off

If you have run the correct cycle more than once and the light still stays on, check:

- that you are using the proper cleaning disc
- that the tablet is suitable for espresso machine cleaning
- that the machine completes the full program without stopping
- that the water tank is full enough during the cycle

If all of that looks right, the issue may be related to the machine's internal tracking or a control fault. At that point, checking the manual or contacting Breville support is the safest next step.

## Final answer

If your **Breville Barista Express Clean Me light won't turn off**, the fix is usually simple: run the **full cleaning cycle correctly** with the **cleaning disc**, **tablet**, and the proper **button combination**.

In most cases, the light stays on because the cycle was interrupted, started the wrong way, or done without the correct setup.
