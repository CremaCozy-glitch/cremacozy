# Cookie Policy

*Last updated: October 2026*

Cookies are small files stored in your browser. This page explains how CremaCozy may use them.

## What we use

- **Essential storage.** We remember in your browser that you dismissed the cookie notice.
- **Analytics.** We use Google Analytics to understand which guides are useful. It sets cookies that collect anonymous usage data.
- **Third-party links.** When you click an Amazon link, Amazon may set its own cookies to track the referral. See Amazon's own policies.

## Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Some features may not work properly if you do.

For more on how we handle information, see our [Privacy Policy]({{ '/privacy-policy/' | relative_url }}).
